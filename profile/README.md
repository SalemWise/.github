### SalemWise Solutions

Private, cloud-agnostic AI for engineering and AEC firms. It runs on AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, or your own servers, with open-weight models and no public AI API.

- **Corpus**: document AI for engineering firms. Answers cite the document and page, and say "nothing on file" when the sources don't support an answer. [Demo on sample documents](https://demo.salemwise.com)
- **Nxivo**: AI phone receptionist, in production with paying small-business customers. Real-time voice, self-hosted LLMs, and a fine-tuned end-of-turn model.
- **TensorSwing**: weekly stock forecasts, hashed before the open and graded in a [public record](https://github.com/YermekIbrayev/tensorswing-record). Informational only.

We also take contract work on RAG and document AI, voice agents, and LLM fine-tuning.
